3. ORGANIZATIONAL OBJECTIVES AND PRINCIPLES (strategy and values)
- AI strategy in one paragraph: use AI to answer simple customer questions faster and free staff for complex and vulnerable customers. AI never makes decisions about a customer's money or supply on its own.
- Our AI principles, each with one example: 1) A human is always reachable (every chatbot answer offers "talk to a person"). 2) We tell people when it is AI (the chatbot says so in its first message). 3) No decisions about people without a human (payment plans, disconnections and debt collection are decided by staff).
- Risk appetite for AI: low for anything touching customers' payments, supply or vulnerable customers; medium for internal productivity.
- Uses of AI we will not allow: automated decisions on disconnection, debt collection or payment plans; emotion recognition; any use of customer data to train vendor models.
